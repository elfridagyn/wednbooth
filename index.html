Still curious?

Quick answers before you hop in.

How do I use this site?

Allow camera access, set your layout, filter, effect, timer, and frame, then press the “AMBIL FOTO” (take photo) button until every slot is filled. Check the results, retake if needed, then download as a JPG or GIF.

Can I change the frame after taking photos?

Yes! You can swap the frame after your session ends, no retakes needed.

Can I change the filter after taking photos?

No. Filters can only be chosen before you take photos, so make sure you’re happy with your pick before starting your session.

Want to start a new session?

Press the Reset button to clear your photos and start fresh.

Is wednbOOth free?

Yes! Totally free, and no account needed.
